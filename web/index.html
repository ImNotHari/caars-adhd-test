Important Medical Disclaimer

The Conners' Adult ADHD Rating Scales (CAARS) is a screening tool designed to assess behavioral symptoms of adult ADHD. An elevated score (T ≥ 65) indicates higher-than-average symptoms, but does not establish a medical or psychiatric diagnosis on its own. A formal diagnosis requires a comprehensive clinical evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional, psychiatrist, or clinical psychologist.
