Medical & Psychometric Notice

The Conners' Adult ADHD Rating Scales (CAARS) is a standardized psychometric instrument designed to quantify the frequency and severity of behavioral symptoms associated with adult ADHD. A high score or clinical elevation (T ≥ 65) indicates clinically significant symptoms compared to the normative population, but does NOT alone establish a medical or psychiatric diagnosis of ADHD. Formal diagnosis requires a comprehensive clinical evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional in accordance with DSM-5 diagnostic criteria, incorporating developmental history, functional impairment, and ruling out differential medical or psychiatric conditions.
